# DialNexa

A voice AI platform that gives businesses human-sounding agents for inbound and outbound phone conversations. Agents hold natural conversations, follow scripts, keep context, qualify leads, answer FAQs, run eligibility checks, schedule meetings, send payment links, and log outcomes into CRMs and internal tools.
